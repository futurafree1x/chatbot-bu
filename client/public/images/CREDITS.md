## hendrix-ahoy-1967-wide.webp, hendrix-ahoy-1967-tall.webp

- **Original:** "Popartiest Jimi Hendrix op Hippy Happy beurs voor jongeren in Ahoy", Rotterdam, 10 November 1967.
- **Photographer:** Ary Groeneveld (attributed). Collectie Ary Groeneveld, Stadsarchief Rotterdam, item 20715-6-32.
- **Licence:** CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication) by the rights holder, Gemeente Rotterdam (Stadsarchief).
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Popartiest_Jimi_Hendrix_op_Hippy_Happy_beurs_voor_jongeren_in_Ahoy.jpg
- **Our changes:** the 1920 px version was trimmed by 1.5% on each edge to remove the scan border, and recoloured with a gradient map (black → deep red → red-orange → orange → pale yellow) in the style of late-60s album art. The "tall" file is a 2:3 crop centred on Hendrix for phone screens. Both are WebP at quality 60.

CC0 does not require attribution, but we credit it anyway, as NFR-22 asks.
